# SourceTX Content Recommendations

The expanded website now presents SourceTX as both a technology talent partner and a technology-services provider. Before publishing, validate every claim against actual delivery capability and client experience.

## Highest-priority additions

1. **Verified proof points** — Add only numbers SourceTX can substantiate, such as active consultants, completed placements, repeat-client rate, average response time, supported locations, or years in business.
2. **Client outcomes** — Publish three short, anonymized case studies: the client challenge, SourceTX team, work completed, and measurable result.
3. **Leadership and recruiter profiles** — Real photographs and short biographies make a staffing company feel credible and approachable.
4. **Technology partnerships** — Display AWS, Azure, GCP, Databricks, Snowflake, ServiceNow, Salesforce, or other partner badges only when SourceTX has documented authorization to use them.
5. **Industry pages** — Add focused pages for financial services, healthcare, automotive/manufacturing, retail, and public sector once SourceTX can support each page with real experience.
6. **Candidate resources** — Add interview preparation, résumé guidance, contractor onboarding, benefits, payroll timing, and recruitment-fraud guidance.
7. **Service-level clarity** — For managed services, publish support hours, response targets, escalation model, governance cadence, and the boundary between SourceTX and client ownership.
8. **Trust and compliance** — Add Equal Employment Opportunity language, accessibility statement, recruitment-fraud warning, data-retention policy, and applicable UK/US privacy language after legal review.

## Recommended homepage proof section

Replace generic claims with a four-number section after verification:
- Technology professionals placed
- Repeat clients
- Average candidate-submission time
- Countries or states supported
